# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Student club design teams (e.g. a college fest's publicity team), used equally by two roles:
- **Junior designers** check a plan (inspiration images + a short description) or a finished poster before showing
  it to seniors, to avoid wasted rounds of rework.
- **Seniors / design heads** record which designs they accepted or rejected (History), and read the Team style
  page to see what the team's look is and how well the model knows it.

Secondary audience: CS698B (FDE1) instructors and peers at the demo on 26 Nov 2026, who judge the ETL, AIML, VIZ,
APP and DEV components.

## Product Purpose

VibeCheck learns a design team's visual style from its own past posts and tells a designer, before seniors see the
work, how on-brand it is: a 0-100 score, a verdict (On-brand / Borderline / Off-brand), plain-language reasons, the
team's closest past posts, and for finished posters a finish checklist. Success: fewer rejected drafts, and seniors'
decisions feed back into the model.

## Positioning

The score is learned from *this team's* posts and *these seniors'* decisions, not a generic "good design" model,
and the app says plainly what it knows and what it does not (approval model off until enough decisions exist).

## Operating Context

Runs locally on a laptop (FastAPI + SQLite backend, React/Vite frontend, CPU models). Data comes from Instagram
import, bulk uploads, other clubs' posts, and designs marked accepted/rejected with a project tag. Other teams add
features through webhook plugins whose panels appear inside our pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Check my idea (plan), Check my design (final), Result, History, Team style, Team setup, Plugins.
- Terms: on-brand score, verdict, reasons, closest posts, finish checklist, approval model, plan vs final,
  decided designs, projects (briefs).
- Approval model switches on only with enough decided designs; when off the UI must say so and why.
- Confidence of a score is shown; the synthetic "Demo Studio" team must always be labelled synthetic.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real team: Techkriti posts (local only, private; never publish).
- Synthetic team: Demo Studio (data_demo/), generated by scripts/make_demo_team.py.
- Held-out results in KNOWN_ISSUES.md and DEMO.md. No testimonials or user studies exist; do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Honest over impressive: show what the model knows, how sure it is, and when it is off.
2. Reasons before numbers: every score comes with words a designer can act on.
3. The team's own work is the reference: show their posts, palette and ranges, not generic rules.
4. Fast check, low ceremony: a junior should get a verdict in a minute.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Must keep: light/dark mode with a toggle, visible keyboard focus, skip link, colourblind-safe colours, and verdicts
always written in words (never colour alone).
